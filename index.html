Frequently Asked Questions

Customer Help 24/7

Are these master grade sneakers? How is the quality? +

Yes. MinimalKicks specializes exclusively in 1:1 master copy sneakers. We use original-grade leather, exact factory stitching, authentic silhouette curvature, and responsive cushioning so your pair feels, weighs, and looks identical to high-heat releases.

Do I get real in-hand photos before my order is dispatched? +

Yes, absolutely! We share real in-hand photos and details of our collection directly on WhatsApp so you can verify the exact quality and colorway before shipping.

How do I choose the correct size? Do they fit TTS? +

Most models fit True to Size (TTS) based on standard UK sizing. You can click "UK Size Guide" on any product card to see EU and CM foot measurements. When in doubt, message us on WhatsApp and our sneaker specialists will advise you.

What payment methods do you accept? Is COD available? +

We accept Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, all UPI apps, and direct Bank Transfer. Partial Cash On Delivery (COD) is also supported across select pin codes. Confirmation is handled instantly in your WhatsApp chat.

How long does delivery take and can I track my parcel? +

Orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours of order confirmation. Delivery across India takes 4–7 business days. As soon as your order ships, an official tracking link from BlueDart, Delhivery, or DTDC is shared on your WhatsApp.

What if the shoes don't fit? What is your exchange policy? +

We offer a hassle-free 7-day doorstep size exchange. If the size is too tight or loose, simply notify us on WhatsApp. As long as the sneakers are unworn with original tags and box, we dispatch your replacement size.

Can you source a sneaker not listed on your website? +

Absolutely! Send us any photo on WhatsApp (+91 7779012100). Our sourcing team will check availability, send live pictures, and provide instant pricing.
